## DIVELUX

We make one thing: [handmade wool felt sauna hats](https://diveluxsaunahat.com). Along the way we build free, open tools and data for people who love the sauna.

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Sauna Session Planner](https://github.com/divelux/sauna-session-planner) | Plan rounds, cool-downs and water for any sauna, with a round timer. [Try it](https://divelux.github.io/sauna-session-planner/) |
| [Sauna Traditions of the World](https://github.com/divelux/sauna-traditions-dataset) | Open reference dataset of 28 sauna and bathing traditions |
| [The Sauna Hat Guide](https://github.com/divelux/sauna-hat-guide) | Free 12-page illustrated PDF and slides |
| [Sauna & Banya Icon Set](https://github.com/divelux/sauna-icons) | 36 line icons in SVG |
| [Sauna hat 3D prints](https://github.com/divelux/sauna-hat-3d-prints) | Drying stand, hat hooks and a ladle hook |
| [30-Day Sauna & Cold Plunge Tracker](https://github.com/divelux/sauna-tracker) | Spreadsheet, printable and Notion template |

All of it is free to use and share. More at [diveluxsaunahat.com/pages/sauna-tools](https://diveluxsaunahat.com/pages/sauna-tools).
